📚 Clinical Endodontics Guide

Stage 1: Pulp Chamber

Sodium Hypochlorite (NaOCl 2.5-5.25%): Dissolves organic pulp tissue.

17% EDTA: Demineralizes the inorganic smear layer.

Stage 2: Apical Danger Zone

Chemical Safety: Never mix NaOCl with Chlorhexidine to avoid toxic Para-chloroaniline (PCA) precipitate.

Side-Vented Needle: Position 2-3mm short of working length.

Stage 3: Medicaments

Calcium Hydroxide [Ca(OH)₂]: High pH ~12.5 releases hydroxyl ions to destroy bacterial membranes.
